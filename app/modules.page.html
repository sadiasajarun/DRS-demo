Four modules — what each must do

RMP

Remote Monitoring Platform

Pulls generation, consumption, export and performance from every inverter through manufacturer cloud APIs, normalises it into one schema, flags anomalies and is the execution channel for halt/resume.

Technical requirements
  • Inverter data via each manufacturer's cloud API: instantaneous power, daily & cumulative kWh, string-level V/I, fault codes, last-communication (§3.2.1)
  • Brand-agnostic normalisation layer — one common schema for all enlisted brands (§3.2.1, §3.9)
  • Halt/resume execution channel; per-brand read / write capability documented before enlistment (§3.2.1)
  • Every command and acknowledgement logged with timestamp, system ID, command type, result — visible to IDCOL (§3.2.1)
Finance & accounts requirements
  • Dashboard for IDCOL, DA and households: generation, capacity, export, inventory, financing, contribution, refinancing, outstanding, repayment (§3.2.2)
  • Retention of daily / monthly / cumulative data against system ID (§3.2.2)
  • Performance ratio, specific yield, availability; cohort benchmarking by district and package; automatic flags for under-performing, non-communicating, recurring fault, not exporting, implausible (§3.2.2)
  • Open interfaces for IDCOL MIS, results verification and independent verifiers (§3.2.2)

Hand-offs: Executes HALT/RESUME decided by CRM; feeds generation data to MIS dashboards, early-warning engine and household screens.
MIS

MIS Database (system of record)

The single record of DAs, equipment, inventory, households, financing structure and credit administration — from manufacturer enlistment to the installed system and its refinancing claim.

Technical requirements
  • Equipment enlistment portal → TSC evaluation → Approved Equipment Catalogue; every application retained (§3.3.1)
  • DA financing application: PSI report per batch → IDCOL Technical Unit verifies → lot and serial list allotted to the DA's inventory (§3.3.1)
  • Serialised inventory walled per DA; status in stock / installed / returned / faulty; a serial can never be on two systems (§3.3.1)
  • Household onboarding: model drop-downs from own stock only, serials from in-stock only, no free text; DA field report mandatory (§3.3.1)
  • Bulk CSV import of batch / serial lists (§5.1)
Finance & accounts requirements
  • Unique IDs and financial master linkage; DA master incl. 3-year highlights, assessments, key persons, ESSF plan (§3.3.2)
  • Equipment cost per system; benchmark price per package; ceiling-price rejection at entry (§3.3.2)
  • DA facility record (limit, tranches, rate, tenor, grace, drawdown, headroom) and separate advance facility (§3.3.2)
  • Security register: BG, personal & corporate guarantees, DSRA, liens, hypothecation, DP note, undated cheques; continuous cover computation with alert / disbursement block (§3.3.2)
  • Traceability Supplier → Batch → Serial → DA → System → Claim; duplicate financing control (§3.3.2)
  • Early-warning engine (collection efficiency, PaR30/90, DSCR, telemetry availability, inspection failure, security shortfall) with severity, owner, escalation; tenor-mismatch report (§3.3.2)

Inspection

Inspection Module

IDCOL's independent physical verification of every installed system against the technical standard — the gate that makes a refinance claim eligible for payment.

Technical requirements
  • Inspector records / scans panel, inverter and battery serials on site; automatic cross-check against onboarding serials; mismatch raises a checklist flag (§3.4.1)
  • Distinct from the DA's own field report — both retained against the system ID, tagged by source (§3.4.1)
  • Digital checklist with mandatory geotagged, time-stamped photos and inspector sign-off; offline-capable mobile app (§3.1, §3.4.2)
Finance & accounts requirements
  • Automatic financial-eligibility determination from IDCOL and DA reports (§3.4.2)
  • Inspection ↔ claim linkage; inspection-based payment trigger (§3.4.2)
  • Claim amount displayed for verification before approval (§3.4.2)
  • Exception / hold mechanism blocks payment until resolved and approved (§3.4.2)
  • Combined disbursement / inspection report (§3.4.2); audit trail of every status change with user, time, action, reason (§3.4.2)

CRM

CRM — household loans, receipts, halt/resume

Tracks every household loan (due, paid, overdue), issues digital receipts for every payment channel, and decides when a system is halted or resumed.

Technical requirements
  • Halt/resume decided by CRM, executed via RMP (§3.5.1)
  • Automatic triggers: overdue not cleared within 10 days of schedule → halt; out of network 10 days → halt, resume on reconnect if not in arrears (§3.1)
  • Manual halt / resume by an authorised IDCOL agent, logged in the same audit trail (§3.5.1)
  • Strict DA data isolation: DA agents, collection agents and households see only their DA's accounts; IDCOL sees all (§3.5.1)
Finance & accounts requirements
  • Household loan ledger linked to DA and system ID; financing structure fields (§3.5.2)
  • Three automatic repayment schedules: Advance, Refinancing (IDCOL→DA), Financing (DA→Household) (§3.5.2)
  • Automatic balances: principal outstanding, due, paid, overdue amount, overdue installments/days (§3.5.2)
  • Payment allocation rules; partial / excess / advance payments; complete payment history; unique digital receipt for every payment incl. cash (§3.5.2, §3.6)
  • Reversal / refund with authorisation and audit — no financial transaction is ever deleted (§3.5.2, §5.2)
  • DA portfolio ledger and IDCOL program-wide view; automated notifications for upcoming, received, overdue, impending halt (§3.5.2, §5.1)
  • Three collection channels, daily 3-way reconciliation (§3.6)
