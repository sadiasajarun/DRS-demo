Inspection

Inspection Queue

IDCOL's independent verification of every installed system. The result determines financial eligibility of the DA's refinance claim.

What the requirement asks of this screen

§3.4 · click to expand
  • System validation and physical verification of installed systems against the digital checklist and the approved technical specification, with photographs, GPS and time stamp (§3.1)
  • Inspector records or scans the serials of panel, inverter and battery found on site; automatic cross-check against the DA's onboarding serials; mismatch raises a checklist flag (§3.4.1)
  • Independent of, and in addition to, the DA's own field report — both retained against the system ID, tagged by source
  • Offline mobile inspection app with digital checklist, mandatory geotagged / time-stamped photos and inspector sign-off (§3.4.2)
