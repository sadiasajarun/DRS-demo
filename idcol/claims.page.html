Refinance Claims & Disbursement

A claim becomes eligible only after the IDCOL inspection passes. Maker prepares, a different checker approves, credit admin disburses — every step audited.
Requirement coverage§3.4.2 · §3.3.2 · §5.2
  • Each inspection record linked to the household / system and the DA refinancing claim; successful inspection makes the claim eligible for onward processing
  • Claim amount displayed alongside the inspection record for verification before approval; reject refinance above the package maximum
  • Exception / hold mechanism prevents payment until discrepancies are resolved and approved
  • Duplicate financing control: the same serial or system can never appear in more than one claim
  • Maker-checker / review-validation-approval-confirmation workflow for financial transactions and claims (§5.2)
  • Combined disbursement / inspection report: DA, system ID, amount, inspection status, approval status, payment status
Acting user: — Review a submitted claim as one user, then switch user (top bar) to Approve; approval by the reviewer is refused, and DAs cannot approve their own claims.
