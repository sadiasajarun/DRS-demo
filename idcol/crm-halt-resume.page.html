What the requirement asks of this screen

§3.1 · §3.5.1 · §3.5.2 · click to expand
  • Connect to the inverter of every DRS system and, where necessary, instruct it to halt or resume operation
  • Halt when the household fails to clear the overdue within 10 days of the repayment schedule; resume when cleared
  • Halt any system out of network for 10 consecutive calendar days; resume once connectivity is restored provided the account is not in arrears
  • Halt / resume decided by the CRM, executed through RMP's inverter integration layer
  • Manual halt or resume by an authorised IDCOL agent at any time, overriding the automatic state — logged in the same audit trail
  • Log every halt and resume with time, trigger and operator, visible to IDCOL
