MIS

Serialised Inventory — Program-wide

Every unit is tracked by serial from supplier lot to installed system. IDCOL sees all DAs; each DA sees only its own units.
Requirement coverage§3.3.1 · §3.3.2
  • Each DA's inventory shows only lots allotted to that DA — walled off from every other DA — by model, with every serial and its status (in stock, installed, returned, faulty)
  • The same serial can never be allocated to more than one household system — enforced at the point of entry
  • Traceability: Supplier → Batch/Lot → Serial → DA → Household/System → Financing/Refinancing claim
  • Returns, defect, replacement and end-of-life register at serial level, linked to warranty claims and ESSF (Li-ion / e-waste) handling
