Complaints / Tickets

Program-wide ticket queue. Households and DAs raise and escalate cases; IDCOL investigates, responds, requests information, returns cases to the DA or resolves them. Financial, inspection and RMP outcomes still follow their own authorised workflows.

  • DA agents and households see only their DA's cases; IDCOL has Program-wide visibility (§3.5.1)
  • Every ticket action recorded in the audit trail with user, time, action and reason (§4)
  • Ticket resolution never bypasses financial approval, inspection approval or RMP authorisation
