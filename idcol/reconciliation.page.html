Reconciliation & Exceptions

Daily three-way reconciliation of payment gateway settlements, bank statements, cash / DA collections and the CRM loan ledger. Pending transfers post only when a matching bank record (or accepted evidence) exists; everything else becomes an exception with an owner.
Requirement coverage§3.6 · §5.2
  • Daily reconciliation between payment gateway settlements, bank statements, cash collection records and CRM / loan ledger records
  • Exception reports for unmatched, duplicate, failed, reversed, refunded or otherwise irregular transactions
  • Authorised financial adjustments without deleting the original transaction — original amount, revised amount, reason, user and approval retained
  • Bank transfers / deposits are posted only after matching (bank reference, amount, account, date) — never treated as reconciled automatically
