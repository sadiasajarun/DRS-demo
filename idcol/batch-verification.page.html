PSI Batch Verification & Allotment

When a DA declares an equipment lot, the PSI (or equivalent) report is verified by the IDCOL Technical Unit; on verification the full serial list is allotted into that DA's walled inventory.
Requirement coverage§3.3.1 · §3.3.2 · §5.1
  • DA declares the approved model(s) and uploads the PSI / equivalent report per lot or batch
  • IDCOL Technical Unit verifies; the lot with its full list of individual serial numbers is allotted into the DA's own inventory
  • Recorded per batch: model, quantity, PSI report and upload, verification status, verifying officer, date, resulting serial list
  • Bulk CSV import of batch / serial lists instead of manual entry (§5.1); duplicate serials rejected at entry
  • Lot linked to the DA financing / advance / refinancing arrangement — amount financed against equipment (§3.3.2)
