Households — System Registry

The core system of record (§3.1): one row per installed system, linking household, DA, equipment serials, inspection, claim, loan and inverter state.

What the requirement asks of this screen

§3.1 MIS · click to expand
  • Unique system identifier, household details, address with GPS, package size, equipment serial numbers, DA name, DA inspection information, installation date, net-metering status
  • Loan terms, disbursement process, repayment schedule, repayment status, payment receipts, due / overdue status
  • Both the DA onboarding report and IDCOL's inspection retained against the system ID, tagged by source (§3.3.1, §3.4.1)
