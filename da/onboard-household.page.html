Onboard household

Five steps — equipment comes only from this DA's in-stock inventory; financing terms come from IDCOL's program configuration.
Requirement coverage§3.3.1 · §3.3.2 · §3.5.2
  • Panel, inverter and battery model fields are drop-downs populated only from this DA's own allotted inventory; free-text model entry is not possible
  • Once a model is selected, only serials currently "in stock" for that model are offered; selected serials are reserved, then move to "installed" and are permanently linked to the system ID on confirmation
  • DA field inspection / installation report (checklist, photographs, installer sign-off) is mandatory before the household record is complete and active
  • Duplicate household / duplicate financing control; contract price above the package ceiling rejected at entry; district must be within the DA's authorised areas
  • Financing rate, tenor, repayment frequency and contribution rules come from IDCOL program configuration; schedule generated automatically; IDCOL inspection requested
